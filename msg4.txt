fix(onboarding): first-visit offer, subject filtering and profile identity

Diagnosed and fixed the defects found by driving the app in a real browser.

- First-visit onboarding never opened: the post-init callback was mangled by an
  encoding conversion in its own comment, so it threw after setting the offer
  flag. It is now ASCII-only, wrapped in try/catch, and a render failure can no
  longer prevent the offer. Returning guests and onboarded students are not
  asked again in the same session.
- The dashboard crashed on first load because subject lookups matched the
  legacy short id after subjects became pack-qualified. getTopic/getSubject now
  accept both a namespaced key and a bare id, and topicKey resolves either.
- Subject tiles and their topic counts matched on the legacy id, so every
  subject stayed visible after deselection. They now match the stable key, so
  the Subjects page, quizzes, revision, materials and priority view all show
  only the selected subjects.
- Onboarding saved the view but not the narrowed selection, so a reload
  restored all subjects while the plan used the chosen four. It now persists
  the selection and the finished profile to the workspace, so the sidebar name,
  initials and heading update immediately instead of after a reload.
- An onboarded guest is labelled with their own name; only a truly anonymous
  visitor sees the generic guest label.

Verified in a headless browser end to end: clean storage offers onboarding;
profile, subject, time and review steps all advance; deselecting two of six
subjects leaves four, and four are stored and rendered on every page; the
dashboard, quiz, revision, subjects and materials pages agree; reloading does
not re-open onboarding; zero console errors.
